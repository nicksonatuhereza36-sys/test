### Design & Structure Context from Screenshot
Replicate the layout, hierarchy, and aesthetic of the attached school/university landing page (green, white, dark accent palette) adapted for St Charles SS Ntungamo:
1. **Top Notice & Contact Bar**: Quick links (Notices, Gallery, Sports, Contact), social links, and official school contact number.
2. **Main Navigation**: School crest / brand "St Charles SS Ntungamo", navigation items (Home, About Us, Academics, Admissions, Students, Facilities, Contact), and call-to-action button.
3. **Hero Section**: High-impact banner with bold typography celebrating academic excellence and holistic education in Ntungamo, slider controls, and clean backdrop.
4. **Admissions Quick Call-to-Action Bar**: Floating card banner announcing O-Level & A-Level admissions with an "Apply Now" button.
5. **About St Charles SS Ntungamo**: Overview of the school's heritage, mission, vision, and core values with supporting imagery and floating stat/achievement badges.
6. **Curriculum & Core Focus Highlight**: Highlighting UNEB curriculum, science and arts programs, and moral discipline.
7. **"What Makes St Charles SS Stand Out"**: 4-column feature grid with clean icons (Shaping Futures, National Curriculum Framework, Academic Excellence, Sports & Co-Curriculars).
8. **Academic Character & Values**: Feature section with layered backdrop and highlight card on discipline, integrity, and leadership.
9. **Latest News & Events**: News grid featuring one main highlighted story and a column of recent updates with dates and thumbnails.
10. **Headteacher / Leadership Message**: Dedicated spotlight section featuring the Headteacher / Board message and vision for the school.
11. **Student & Community Voices**: Testimonials section for alumni, parents, and students.
12. **School Magazine / Publications Banner**: Prominent green banner for the annual school publication / achievements report.
13. **Affiliations & Partners**: Section showing affiliations (Ministry of Education and Sports, UNEB, sports federations).
14. **Pre-Footer CTA**: School motto ("Education for impact, leadership, and service") with direct application link.
15. **Full Dark Footer**: Location details (Ntungamo, Uganda), contacts, department links, student resources, social media links, and floating WhatsApp support widget.
